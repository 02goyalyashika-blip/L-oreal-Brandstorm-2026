# Project Overview

## Executive Summary

**Velum** was developed for L'Oréal Brandstorm 2026 as a strategic innovation concept exploring how fragrance could evolve from projection toward intimacy.

The team framed the opportunity around a central question:

> **What can L'Oréal do to move beyond “spraying” to “infusing”?**

The answer was a proposed infusion-first ritual combining fragrance, skin science, beauty technology, personalization and a refill-led luxury ecosystem.

## The Strategic Problem

The final submission identifies unmet needs in traditional fragrance around:

1. **Longevity** — consumers want fragrance to remain noticeable for longer.
2. **Personalization** — consumers increasingly value an experience that feels individual to them.
3. **Technology-driven safety and differentiation** — consumers are open to more advanced beauty experiences and new formats.

The team's reframing was deliberately experience-led: rather than simply improving a conventional spray, redesign how fragrance meets the skin.

## Concept

Velum is a proposed luxury fragrance platform built around a one-time, guided application ritual. The concept combines:

- alcohol-free diffusion;
- a nano-diffusion “stamp” applicator;
- surface-level anchoring within the stratum corneum;
- fragrance adaptation to individual skin chemistry;
- integrated UV-C sanitation; and
- refillable, long-life hardware.

The final deck describes the intended experience as **“a scent that lives with you, not in the air.”**

## Strategic Fit

The submission explicitly maps Velum to four L'Oréal R&I priorities:

- **Beauty Tech & Digital Services**
- **Green Sciences & Sustainability**
- **Inclusive & Personalized Beauty**
- **Skin Science & Beauty**

This positioning allows the concept to connect consumer demand with both product innovation and wider portfolio priorities.

## Commercial Logic

The proposed model is a **hero product + refills** system supported by a DTC-led route to market and selective retail distribution. The concept targets Gen Z, Millennials and “Exception luxury” consumers, with a geographic framing across Europe, North America and Asia-Pacific.

The final deck includes concept-stage commercial assumptions around pricing, gross margin, break-even and market capture. These should be interpreted as competition-stage assumptions rather than independently validated financial projections.

## What This Project Demonstrates

Velum demonstrates an end-to-end innovation process spanning:

**Consumer need → opportunity framing → concept development → technology proposition → sustainability model → commercial strategy → pitch storytelling**

The repository therefore presents the work as a portfolio case study, while preserving the distinction between the team's submitted concept and independently validated evidence.
