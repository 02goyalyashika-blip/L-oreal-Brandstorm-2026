# My Contribution

## Why This Page Exists

This repository presents a team project. For a job-application portfolio, it is important to distinguish the overall team concept from the work personally led or contributed by the individual portfolio owner.

## Contribution Areas

The following categories reflect the capability areas demonstrated by the project and should be finalized against the work actually completed by the portfolio owner:

### Consumer Insight

Contributed to identifying and synthesizing the consumer tensions around fragrance longevity, personalization and premium experience that shaped the opportunity space.

### Innovation & Concept Development

Contributed to the development and refinement of the Velum proposition, including the move from conventional spraying toward an infusion-first ritual.

### Business Strategy

Contributed to framing the hero-product + refill model, target consumer, go-to-market logic and commercial narrative presented in the final submission.

### Storytelling & Presentation

Contributed to structuring the final story from consumer problem through solution, strategic fit and business potential.

## Before Using This Repository in Applications

Replace or edit the sections above so they describe **only the work personally performed**. Do not claim ownership of research, financial modelling, product design, presentation work or pitch delivery unless it was genuinely your contribution.

A strong final version should answer three questions:

1. **What did I personally do?**
2. **What decisions did my work influence?**
3. **What skills did the project demonstrate?**

## Suggested Resume Translation

Once the contribution is confirmed, the final repository can support one or two resume bullets using the structure:

**Action + scope + strategic outcome**

For example, the project could be framed around translating consumer insight into a differentiated fragrance innovation concept and commercial model — but the exact wording should be finalized only after confirming the portfolio owner's actual responsibilities.
