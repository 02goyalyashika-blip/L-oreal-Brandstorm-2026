# Research & Evidence Notes

## Purpose

This page records the evidence used in the final L'Oréal Brandstorm 2026 submission and clarifies what is **source evidence**, what is **team framing**, and what remains a **concept-stage assumption**.

## Evidence in the Final Submission

The final presentation uses the following consumer and market signals:

- **76%** — cited as the share of consumers prioritizing longevity.
- **65%** — cited as the share of consumers demanding natural ingredients and ethical sourcing.
- Qualitative social-media observations around fragrance reapplication, interest in non-alcoholic long-wear formats, and personalized scent identity.
- A proposed premium audience of **125–130 million** across Europe, North America and Asia-Pacific.

These figures and observations are preserved here exactly as part of the competition submission's evidence base. The deck does not provide a full methodology, sample size, fieldwork dates, or underlying datasets, so this repository does **not** present them as independently validated market research.

## Consumer-Insight Sources Shown in the Deck

The presentation attributes qualitative observations to Reddit and Instagram users. They are used as directional signals illustrating consumer tensions around:

- the need to reapply fragrance after a few hours;
- interest in non-alcoholic fragrance with extended wear; and
- desire for a scent that feels personally distinctive.

Because the final deck does not include post links, usernames, collection dates, or a reproducible sampling method, the repository preserves these as **research observations shown in the submitted deck**, rather than reconstructing or fabricating original source records.

## Concept Assumptions

The following are not treated as established external facts; they are propositions or targets from the team's final submission:

- 12+ hour intended wear;
- approximately 500 rounded nano-tips at ≤0.1 mm;
- surface-level diffusion within the stratum corneum;
- UV-C sanitation integrated into the cap;
- refill-driven hardware designed for multi-year circular use;
- 60–80% indicative gross margin;
- $275–$325 indicative ASP;
- ~$120 indicative cost;
- Year 1–2 break-even target.

These statements describe the **proposed Velum concept** and its commercial model, not independently verified product performance, safety validation, regulatory approval, manufacturing feasibility, or market results.

## Evidence Discipline

For portfolio use, the repository follows three labels mentally even where the original deck does not:

**Evidence** — a consumer or market signal explicitly presented in the submission.

**Concept** — a proposed product, experience, technology or business-model element created by the team.

**Assumption** — a numerical target or commercial estimate used to make the competition concept tangible.

Maintaining this distinction makes the case study more credible in interviews and avoids overstating what was proven during the competition.
