# Solution Design

## 01. Design Principle

Velum is built around one core shift:

> **Transform fragrance from projection to intimacy.**

Rather than treating fragrance as a product that is simply sprayed onto skin, the concept proposes a guided ritual intended to anchor scent at the skin surface and release it more gradually.

## 02. Experience Journey

### 1 — Preparation

The concept proposes an **alcohol-free diffusion** approach using skin-compatible lipids/ceramides instead of harsh alcohol solvents.

**Design intent:** create a fragrance format that supports a more skin-anchored experience.

### 2 — Application

The proposed **Nano-Diffusion Applicator** uses approximately 500 rounded nano-tips, each ≤0.1 mm, arranged as a luxury “stamp” ritual.

The final submission describes the proposed mechanism as:

- surface-level diffusion;
- no dermal penetration;
- no systemic exposure; and
- non-invasive, cosmetic-grade technology.

These statements describe the team's competition concept and should not be interpreted as independently validated technical or safety claims.

### 3 — Infusion

The concept proposes that fragrance is held within the **stratum corneum** for controlled, intimate release over time.

The intended consumer experience changes from:

**instant projection → gradual revelation**

### 4 — Adaptation

Velum is positioned around **“Beauty for Each”**: scent is intended to evolve differently according to individual skin chemistry.

The deck also proposes cosmetic-grade neuro-aromatic accords designed to evoke perceptions such as calm, focus or freshness through scent.

### 5 — Post-Use

The applicator concept includes **integrated UV-C light in the cap**, designed to sanitize the tips after each session.

This supports the team's wider positioning of Velum as a technology-enhanced luxury ritual rather than simply a new fragrance formula.

## 03. Product System

Velum is not presented as a standalone fragrance bottle. It is a proposed ecosystem consisting of:

**Hero product + refill cartridges + reusable hardware + experiential retail / DTC touchpoints**

The final submission describes the hardware as refillable and designed for multi-year circular use, with selected bio-based aroma fractions intended to reduce petrochemical dependency.

## 04. Experience Design Logic

The solution brings together four layers:

| Layer | Role in the experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Fragrance** | Creates the sensory expression |
| **Skin science** | Frames surface-level anchoring and controlled release |
| **Beauty tech** | Enables the proposed application and hygiene ritual |
| **Circular design** | Extends hardware life through refills |

The strategic strength of the proposition is the integration of these layers into a single consumer ritual.

## 05. Why the Format Matters

The innovation is not only the proposed applicator. The more important shift is behavioral:

**Spray → Stamp**  
**Projection → Intimacy**  
**Single-use packaging → Refill ecosystem**  
**One-size-fits-all scent experience → Personalized skin expression**

## 06. Design Trade-Offs & Validation Needs

Because Velum is a competition-stage concept, several areas would require validation before commercialization:

- formulation stability and compatibility with the proposed applicator;
- actual fragrance retention and release performance;
- cosmetic safety and regulatory compliance;
- durability and cleaning performance of the hardware;
- user acceptance of the stamping ritual;
- manufacturing economics at scale; and
- real-world refill behavior.

These are not gaps in the original pitch so much as the logical next steps for converting a strategic concept into a testable innovation program.
