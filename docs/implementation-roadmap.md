# Implementation Roadmap

## From Competition Concept to Testable Pilot

Velum is a concept-stage proposal. The next step is not immediate scale; it is to reduce the uncertainty around the highest-risk assumptions through staged validation.

## Phase 1 — Consumer Validation

**Objective:** Test whether the proposed ritual solves a meaningful consumer problem.

Key questions:

- Does the infusion-first ritual feel more desirable than conventional spraying?
- Which benefit matters most: longevity, personalization, intimacy, or sustainability?
- Does the ritual feel premium, intuitive and worth repeating?

**Output:** validated consumer proposition, priority benefit and early willingness-to-try / willingness-to-pay signals.

## Phase 2 — Technical & Formulation Feasibility

**Objective:** Establish whether the proposed delivery mechanism can work safely and consistently.

Key workstreams:

- formulation compatibility;
- applicator engineering;
- skin-barrier interaction testing;
- hygiene and UV-C validation;
- dosage consistency;
- stability and shelf-life testing.

The competition concept's claims around nano-tips, stratum-corneum anchoring, 12+ hour wear and UV-C sanitation should be treated as **hypotheses requiring technical, safety and regulatory validation**.

## Phase 3 — Luxury Pilot

**Objective:** Introduce a tightly controlled version of Velum to a limited premium audience.

Possible pilot architecture:

**Hero product + limited fragrance assortment + refill programme + experiential retail / DTC launch**

Track:

- trial-to-purchase conversion;
- repeat usage;
- refill adoption;
- NPS;
- customer acquisition cost;
- average order value;
- early retention;
- operational failure rates.

## Phase 4 — Business Model Validation

**Objective:** Test whether the proposed economics work at real unit and customer level.

Validate:

- hardware COGS;
- refill economics;
- gross margin;
- service / sanitation costs;
- acquisition cost;
- refill frequency;
- lifetime value;
- retail versus DTC economics.

The competition deck's projected margins, ROI and revenue should be replaced with observed pilot data once available.

## Phase 5 — Scale

Only after consumer, technical and economic validation should Velum move toward broader expansion.

**Pilot → validate → refine → expand channels → scale ecosystem**

## Strategic Principle

The roadmap deliberately protects the strongest part of the concept — its distinctive consumer experience — while making the technical and commercial assumptions testable one at a time.

This turns Velum from a competition idea into a **testable innovation thesis**.
