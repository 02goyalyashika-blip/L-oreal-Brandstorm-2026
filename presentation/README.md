# Final Presentation

This folder is reserved for the **Three Amigos — L'Oréal Brandstorm 2026** final submission deck.

## Included material

**File:** `Three_Amigos_L-Oreal_Brandstorm_2026_Presentation.pdf`

The deck contains the team's final three-page submission covering:

1. **Market gap and consumer demand** — longevity, personalization, technology-driven safety, natural ingredients and ethical sourcing.
2. **Velum solution** — preparation, application, infusion, adaptation and post-use hygiene, with proposed nano-diffusion and UV-C sanitation.
3. **Commercial strategy** — target market, hero product + refills, DTC-led go-to-market, indicative economics and growth KPIs.

The PDF remains the authoritative record of the team's final competition submission; the Markdown case-study pages interpret and organize that material for portfolio readers.
